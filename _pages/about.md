I'm a second-year PhD student at the [School of Operations Research and Information Engineering](https://www.duffield.cornell.edu/orie/){:target="\_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener"} at Cornell, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Paul Gölz](https://paulgoelz.de/){:target="\_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener"}.
My recent work addresses the problem of selecting citizens' assemblies while maximizing randomness. I am broadly interested in social choice problems and combinatorial optimization.

Previously, I completed my undergrad in Molecular Sciences and my master's in Computer Science at the University of São Paulo. During my master's, I was advised by [Yoshiko Wakabayashi](https://www.ime.usp.br/~yw/){:target="\_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener"}. Also, I spent a semester at the University of Waterloo working with [Joseph Cheriyan](https://www.math.uwaterloo.ca/~jcheriya/){:target="\_blank" rel="external nofollow noopener"} on approximation algorithms for the 2-edge-connected spanning subgraph problem.
